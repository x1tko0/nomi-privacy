Nomi

Private Offline AI Chat — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your conversations never leave your phone. Nomi runs its AI model entirely on your device. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it technically cannot send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs.

Data the app stores — only on your device

  • Your chats and Nomi's answers (except incognito chats, which are never saved).
  • Facts you ask Nomi to remember ("memory"), your saved prompts, your own personas, actions and personalities.
  • Settings (theme, sounds, answer length, auto-delete, app lock, etc.).
  • Whether you purchased Nomi Pro and when your free Pro trial started.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. Android system backup is disabled for Nomi, so your chats are not copied to cloud backups. You can export a backup file yourself — it goes only where you choose to save it.

Voice

When you use voice input, your speech is recognized on the device by the Android on-device speech recognizer. Audio is not recorded or stored by Nomi. Answers are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech. The microphone permission is requested only when you first tap the microphone.

AI models

The AI models are delivered by Google Play together with the app (Play Asset Delivery). Nomi itself never downloads anything from the Internet. Models run locally; your messages are never used to train any model.

Purchases

Nomi Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Microphone — voice input, recognized on the device.
  • Biometrics — optional app lock (Pro).
  • Notifications — only "model is ready" and one reminder a day before your free Pro trial ends.
  • Billing — the Pro purchase through Google Play.

Nomi does not request Internet, location, contacts, camera, photos or files access.

Deleting your data

Use Privacy → Delete everything in the app, enable auto-delete, delete single chats, or uninstall the app. Since nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

Nomi is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
